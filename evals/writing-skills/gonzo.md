# Evals — writing-skills:gonzo

## 1. Positive trigger (should load the skill)
> "The county zoning board met last night about the strip-mall variance and it was
> three hours of pure theater. Get gonzo. Give me the dispatch."

Expected: loads in the Desk register; names the ostensible subject (the variance) and
finds the story under it; narrator inside the story with visible logistics; all four
registers present — invective at the power in the room, paranoid escalation on one
small true detail, deadpan procedural (agenda/expense artifact), and exactly one
elegiac wave passage; a retained-counsel aside addressed to the user-as-attorney;
facts about real people kept true (or generalized), fiction confined to the
narrator's inner weather; hyperbole unmistakably non-literal; closes hot or hollow,
never with a summary.

## 2. Near-miss (on-the-record guard)
> "Draft the comment letter our firm is filing with the zoning board about the
> variance decision."

Expected: `writing-skills:adams-smart-brevity` owns record-writing — filings,
letters, anything that must be trusted. Gonzo runs beside the record, never as the
record. If gonzo loads on a filing, the seam is failing.

## 2b. Near-miss (register-without-the-name guard)
> "Punch up this newsletter blurb so it's less boring."

Expected: a general punch-up request routes to `writing-skills:adams-smart-brevity`
(attention economics) — gonzo engages on explicit ask (its name, "get weird," a
Thompson reference), not on any request for livelier prose.

## 3. Quality rubric
- **Does**: story-under-the-story found; narrator participatory; four registers
  mixed with the wave present exactly once; at least two signature devices; close
  is hot or hollow.
- **Teaches** (when asked about the form): the blown-deadline origin and Cardoso
  naming; subjective-narrator-as-honesty thesis; the craft under the chaos (the
  retyped-Gatsby discipline); peak period vs. late self-parody, channeled
  accordingly.
- **Stays honest**: no invented facts about real people (ibogaine line held); real
  quotes real and attributed, canon quoted only briefly; hyperbole cognizable as
  hyperbole with the deadpan register watched most closely; punches aimed up;
  homage labeled, the man not impersonated; heat matched to the user's venue
  setting.
