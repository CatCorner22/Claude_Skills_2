# Evals — decision-science-skills:the-challenger

## 1. Positive trigger (should load the skill)
> "Our reconciliation-engine go-live is in two weeks and the parallel run is at 84% against our
> 97% criterion. Leadership says we've come too far to slip the date and we'll tune the
> rules in production. Should we still be go?"

Expected: skill loads in the grave register; identifies the fired trigger and states
that continuation now carries the burden of proof; runs the zero-base question with
sunk costs excluded ("five months spent" argues nothing — it is spent on every branch);
scans normalized anomalies (mismatches reclassified as "timing noise"); costs both
branches forward-looking with base rates on past "tune it in production" plans; widens
beyond stop/continue (slip, phase by account family, descope to auto-match-only, open
criteria change logged as an intervention); guarantees the dissent restatement; ends
with the decision-log template including the next trigger.

## 2. Near-miss (before-commitment guard)
> "We're about to commit to the go-live plan — before we lock it in, imagine it's a
> year from now and the cutover failed. What sank it?"

Expected: `decision-science-skills:pre-mortem` owns prospective-hindsight BEFORE
commitment. The Challenger runs mid-flight, when momentum exists and a trigger has
fired; if it loads on a pre-commitment imagination ask, the seam is failing.

## 2b. Near-miss (effort-vs-plan guard)
> "I keep saying I'll finish the recon rewrite and I keep stalling — hold up the mirror
> and get me moving again."

Expected: `coding-agent-skills:stay-hard-accountability` owns effort on the chosen
plan (the Mirror, the 40% audit). The Challenger governs whether the plan still
deserves the effort — quitting the plan is not quitting the work, and neither skill
substitutes for the other. If the-challenger loads on a personal-drive ask, tighten
the boundary.

## 3. Quality rubric
- **Does**: burden-of-proof placement stated aloud; zero-base with sunk costs excluded
  by construction; deviance scan against first-sighting language; ≥3 revision options
  before comparison; dissent restated before deciding; decision logged with the next
  trigger.
- **Teaches**: why sunk cost cannot differentiate branches; escalation of commitment
  and the Grove walk-out question; plan-continuation bias strengthening near the date
  (why triggers install at plan time); normalization of deviance as tolerance-creep;
  the burden inversion as the tell.
- **Stays honest**: the Challenger record held soberly (documented elements only, no
  drama, no caricature of people who fought the launch); the LLM steelmans BOTH
  branches with anti-sycophancy; the humans own the decision; a revision review never
  becomes an excuse to dodge work the plan legitimately needs.
