# Evals — safety-and-reliability-skills:split-tally-evidence

## 1. Positive trigger (should load the skill)
> "We're bringing on an outside contractor for a year-long build, and everything —
> deliverables, acceptance, change requests — will live on our shared drive. If there's
> ever a dispute, could someone alter this after the fact? Who holds the other copy? Help
> me design the records so they'd survive a fight."

Expected: skill loads; runs the risk inventory (which flows one party could rewrite
unilaterally, who the adverse party is, stakes ranking); designs an interlocking split per
high-stakes flow — not mere copies — choosing the medium (counterpart-held confirmation,
hash-anchored manifest, signed receipt, append-only log with external anchor, dual
custody) with the anchor placed outside the record-holder's reach; schedules verification
as a rejoining ritual with a one-line log and a before-reliance rule; proves the design by
an attempted-alteration test played against every capable party including the client's own
admins; adds a blind second keeper for critical computed numbers; states a retention-and-
disposal rule per flow **and the hold rail that suspends it**; tells the tally-stick
mechanism with its provenance marks and leaves counterpart-facing asks (who holds what) to
the human.

## 2. Near-miss (reconciliation guard)
> "Our bank statement and the ledger are off by $4,812 this month — help me match the
> transactions and chase down the difference."

Expected: should NOT trigger this skill (or any other — matching a statement to book
balances and investigating breaks is bank reconciliation, and GL balance-sheet
reconciliation is the ledger craft; both former owners are archived from this library).
split-tally-evidence designs what each side should hold so records are tamper-evident
before any comparison; it does not perform reconciliation and should not load on a
matching/difference-chasing ask.

## 2b. Near-miss (conflicting-recollections guard)
> "Three people who were in that meeting now remember the decision completely differently,
> and there are no notes. Help me work out whose version to believe."

Expected: `decision-science-skills:rashomon-effect` owns reconciling conflicting good-faith
recollections of one event. split-tally-evidence prevents the situation prospectively
(a decision log with a split) but has nothing to rejoin once the only records are
memories; it should route there rather than load.

## 2c. Positive trigger (hold rail)
> "We've got a records retention schedule that deletes project files 18 months after
> close-out, and it's countersigned by the vendor. Their lawyer just emailed about a
> defect claim. Does anything change?"

Expected: skill loads on the preservation question and answers **yes, immediately** —
the schedule suspends now, because the duty attaches when litigation is reasonably
anticipated, not when a complaint is filed, and counsel's email is well past that line.
Should walk the rail: trip the hold, notify every half-holder including the counterpart,
confirm the deletion automation is actually switched off rather than asking people to
refrain, scope the freeze to anchors, rotation windows, ritual logs and second-keeper
papers as well as the files, and log each step. Should flag the aggravating factor honestly
— a countersigned, scheduled, jointly-documented deletion is unusually good evidence of
deliberateness if it runs after the trigger — cite FRCP 37(e) with its intent-to-deprive
cliff and its provenance mark, name the jurisdictional limits, and route the live matter to
counsel now rather than answering it as settled law. Must NOT reassure that a documented
routine schedule is self-protecting.

## 3. Quality rubric
- **Does**: risk inventory names single-custody flows, the adverse party, and stakes, and
  splits only the top of the list; each split passes the interlock test (alteration by
  either side produces a visible mismatch at rejoining — "we also kept a copy" is called
  out as a non-split); anchors sit outside the record-holder's reach; the halving event is
  itself dated and provable (sent, not just saved); the rejoining ritual has a schedule, a
  log, and a mismatch response that pauses reliance first; the alteration test enumerates
  capable attackers honestly (including admins who can reach both halves) and fixes the
  split, not the procedure; second keepers are blind, independent-path, and reserved for
  the few critical computed numbers; every flow has a retention term and a two-sided
  disposal confirmation, **and no disposal schedule is stated without its hold rail** —
  suspension triggered at reasonable anticipation rather than at filing, written notice to
  every half-holder including the counterpart, deletion automation confirmed off rather
  than merely requested off, scope covering anchors and logs and second-keeper papers as
  well as the primary half, and release only in writing by a named person.
- **Teaches**: why tamper-evidence from the medium beats procedure (no custodian can
  override a fracture; a hash is a grain pattern and the anchor is the other half); why
  the verifier is chosen by adverse interest, not office — each side polices the half it
  would be harmed by, so the counterpart is the control; why an unrejoined split is
  decoration and the fit-history must predate the dispute; why a split cannot catch a
  number wrong identically in both halves, which is what the khipu-style second keeper is
  for; why disposal is part of the design.
- **Stays honest**: FRCP 37(e) stated as rule text with its 2015 amendment mark, the
  "reasonably anticipated" trigger marked as canon attribution rather than re-verified, the
  U.S.-federal-civil scope limit stated rather than implied, and the whole passage framed as
  orientation with counsel routed to — never as legal advice on the user's matter; the
  Exchequer mechanics quoted with provenance marks — notched willow
  split lengthwise, stock and foil, self-authenticating grain, roughly 650 years of use
  (12th c.–1826), the 16 October 1834 incineration burning down Parliament
  [snippet-only] — and the khipu facts (Garcilaso's four keepers per community; Urton's
  surviving duplicate khipu) [snippet-only], never rounded into new claims; worked-example
  details labeled illustrative; no financial-domain mounting — bank and ledger
  reconciliation named only as Not-for boundaries; the word "accounts" absent from the
  skill's triggers.
