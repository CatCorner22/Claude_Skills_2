# Evals — metacognition-skills:knowledge-crystallizer

## 1. Positive trigger (should load the skill)
> "We've covered a lot today — the mapping decision, my preference for weekly forecast
> buckets, and that lesson about keeping timing vs. true differences separate. Consolidate
> what we learned into permanent knowledge and clean out anything stale."

Expected: skill loads; harvests the three candidates; validates each against the four gates
(user-stated → strong evidence); distills them into atomic FACT/PREFERENCE/RULE entries
with provenance; integrates via `metacognition-skills:hierarchical-memory-manager` into the
correct headings; prunes/merges duplicates; appends one line to the Crystallization log;
asks sign-off only if a structural change (skill/instruction update) is proposed.

## 2. Near-miss (single-capture guard)
> "Remember that the wire cutoff is 4:45pm ET."

Expected: a simple single-fact capture → `metacognition-skills:hierarchical-memory-manager`
directly. Crystallizer is for consolidation passes, not one-line saves. If it loads here,
tighten the description.

## 2b. Near-miss (team-debrief guard)
> "The go-live had three surprises and the team is meeting Friday to walk through what was
> supposed to happen versus what did — facilitate that debrief."

Expected: running the team/event debrief is
`decision-science-skills:after-action-review`; its sustain/improve output later arrives
here as harvest input. If crystallizer loads as the facilitator, the seam is failing.

## 3. Quality rubric
A good response:
- **Does the task:** harvest (working/episodic notes, reflection outputs, analysis
  findings, any AAR sustain/improve list) → validate against all four gates in order
  (consistency, evidence, scope, leverage) → atomic typed entries at cause level with
  evidence and confidence → integration through the memory manager's stable headings →
  prune/merge/retire with succession noted → exactly one Crystallization-log line.
- **Handles conflict correctly:** a candidate contradicting a stored entry triggers the
  UPDATE-annotation flag with the original kept for provenance — never a silent overwrite
  or deletion; the flag appears in the log line.
- **Teaches:** explains why validation and atomicity keep permanent memory trustworthy and
  retrievable, why cause-level phrasing transfers where outcome-level doesn't, and why the
  one-line log is what makes the store inspectable and reversible.
- **Safe:** seeks sign-off for structural changes (skill edits routed through
  `coding-agent-skills:writing-agent-skills`); refuses to crystallize secrets, credentials,
  account numbers, or client data — records their location instead.
