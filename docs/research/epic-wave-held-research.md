## Lane 1 — Operational wisdom of previous societies

# Research report — operational wisdom of previous societies (ranked best-first)

All findings below are **[snippet-only]** provenance: WebSearch result blocks with named sources; my one direct WebFetch (kiwihellenist.blogspot.com) was egress-blocked. Snippets were cross-checked across multiple independent results where marked.

**1. Polynesian/Micronesian wayfinding (etak)** — Pacific Islanders, ~1000 BCE–present; revival 1976.
DOCUMENTED: Hokule'a's 1976 Hawaii→Tahiti run (~2,500 nmi, 31 days) navigated instrument-free by Mau Piailug (Weriyeng school, Satawal; trained from age four); landfall at Mataiva predicted a day out by three navigators using different methods. Etak analyzed academically (Edwin Hutchins, distributed cognition; David Lewis, *We, the Navigators*). Nothing legendary here — the rare case where the romantic version is the documented one. [snippet-only, multiple sources incl. archive.hokulea.com]
MECHANISM: No single signal is trusted; position is a continuously re-estimated belief fused from many weak, independent cues (star bearings, swell trains, birds, clouds), held as a *relationship* ("which star is the reference island under now"), not a coordinate. Degrades gracefully — overcast kills stars, swells remain.
PAIRING (pre-directive): Operating when dashboards are down or lying — estimate today's position from weak independent signals with explicit confidence bands; weak-signal early warning before a break is visible.
LLM AMPLIFICATION: Humans fuse 2–3 signals badly under stress; an LLM can enumerate every available weak signal, weight and reconcile them, and *say which cue it's leaning on* — the navigator's running commentary, on demand.
TRIGGER: "feed is down," "I don't trust this number," "estimate blind," "what would we expect to see today."

**2. Split tally sticks** — English Exchequer, 12th c.–1826.
DOCUMENTED: Willow sticks notched for the amount, split lengthwise; creditor kept the longer "stock," debtor the "foil"; the unique wood grain made the pair self-authenticating — forgery required refitting a fracture. Used ~650 years; their careless incineration burned down Parliament (16 Oct 1834). [snippet-only: Science Museum Group, Wikipedia, Christie's]
MECHANISM: The record is *physically halved between adverse parties* — verification is not comparing two ledgers but rejoining two artifacts neither party can alter alone. Tamper-evidence comes free from the medium; the counterparty is the control.
PAIRING (pre-directive): counterpart-held tamper-evident evidence — confirmations, dual-custody records, hash-anchored exports, "does our half fit their half" design.
LLM AMPLIFICATION: Designing what the "two halves" should be for a digital process (checksums, counterpart confirmations, immutable exports) is a design task analysts skip; an LLM can propose the split for any record flow.
TRIGGER: "counterparty confirmation," "tamper-evident," "who holds the other copy," "could someone alter this after the fact."

**3. Shikinen Sengu (Ise Grand Shrine)** — Japan, 690 CE–present (63rd rebuild concludes 2033).
DOCUMENTED: Full reconstruction every 20 years since Empress Jitō (690); a miyadaiku carpenter participates 2–3 times per lifetime (learn→lead→teach); ~30 rituals over ~8 years; 200-year in-house forestry plan (since 1923) growing its own hinoki. HONESTY POINT: the cycle *broke* — a ~120-year lapse after the Ōnin War (Inner Shrine 1462→1585, revived by fundraising nuns of Keikō-in + Oda Nobunaga's backing) — so the lesson includes "the renewal cycle itself needs a funding owner." Shinise bonus: Kongō Gumi, temple builders founded 578, independent until 2006 liquidation (now Takamatsu subsidiary) — longevity via single-craft focus and flexible succession. [snippet-only: japanfs.org, Smithsonian, woodcentral, Wikipedia, INSEAD]
MECHANISM: Knowledge survives only if *exercised on a schedule shorter than a career*: rebuild the real thing, with the last builders alive to correct the next ones. The artifact is disposable; the capability is the asset. Corollary: budget the renewal or it silently lapses.
PAIRING (pre-directive): DR/restore drills that actually rebuild; rebuild-the-app-from-docs-alone exercises (dental app); bus-factor rotation; "could a new hire run the month from the runbook."
LLM AMPLIFICATION: Rebuilding-as-practice is prohibitively expensive in labor — the barrier Ise paid 13 centuries of treasure to clear. An LLM makes rehearsal cheap: simulate the rebuild, interrogate the docs for gaps, play the apprentice who only has the written record.
TRIGGER: "if she left tomorrow," "restore drill," "are the docs enough to recreate this," "it only lives in his head."

**4. Sortition + euthynai; Venetian doge election** — Athens 5th–4th c. BCE; Venice 1268–1797.
DOCUMENTED: Every Athenian magistrate, no exceptions (even strategoi), underwent end-of-term euthynai: a financial *logos* before ten allotted logistai, then general conduct review before ten allotted euthynoi, within a month of leaving office (Oxford Classical Dictionary; Aristotle *Ath. Pol.*). Venice 1268 protocol: ten alternating rounds of lot and vote (30→9→40→12→25→9→45→11→41), blind draws by a random boy (ballotino), family quotas, no campaigning — ran unchanged 529 years. [snippet-only: oxfordre.com, Molinari paper, researchgate protocol analysis]
MECHANISM: Two distinct controls: (a) review is *universal and scheduled by default*, not triggered by suspicion — so no one can infer "why me?"; (b) randomness at multiple interleaved stages makes capture require corrupting the whole pool, not a known gatekeeper. Anti-gaming via unpredictability of *who reviews*, not just what.
PAIRING (pre-directive): random sampling of approvals/changes as anti-gaming control; rotating who reviews whom; end-of-role handover reviews as default, not exception. Distinct from red-teaming: selection design, not adversarial testing.
LLM AMPLIFICATION: Random sampling dies of workload — nobody has time to genuinely review 30 random items monthly. An LLM does the first-pass review of every sampled item, making true universality affordable.
TRIGGER: "spot-check by lot," "sample selection," "they know which ones get looked at," "same person always reviews," "end-of-term handover."

**5. Hammurabi §229–233 + the arch legend** — Babylon ~1754 BCE; internet ~2004 CE.
DOCUMENTED: The stele text is real and graduated: builder dies if owner dies (§229), builder's son if owner's son (§230), replace slave (§231), rebuild at own cost for property/defects (§232–233). LEGEND: the "Roman engineer stood under the arch at de-scaffolding" is *modern folklore* — Kiwi Hellenist traces the earliest instance to a ~2004 USENET signature line; no ancient source; implausible on its face. Great example of a legend the library can hold honestly. Taleb (*Skin in the Game*, 2018) treats §229 as the oldest written rule against transferring hidden tail risk — symmetry by statute. [snippet-only: ehammurabi.org, kiwihellenist (via search), smithcurrie]
MECHANISM: Bind the decision-maker's payoff to the tail outcome *before* the work: the party who creates hidden risk must be the one standing under it when it lands. Graduation matters — consequence scales with harm class.
PAIRING (pre-directive): consequence mapping ("who personally attests this?"); vendor contracts with real defect-liability clauses (dental app subcontractors); named-owner attestations vs. diffuse "reviewed by team."
LLM AMPLIFICATION: Drafting graduated consequence/attestation language is legal-flavored work analysts avoid; an LLM can draft the symmetry table (harm class → who bears what) for any process or contract in minutes.
TRIGGER: "who signs their name to this," "vendor won't stand behind it," "approval with no accountability," "who eats the loss."

**6. Venetian Arsenal** — Venice, 1104–1797 (peak 16th c.).
DOCUMENTED: ~16,000 workers; standardized galley dimensions and interchangeable parts held in inventory; 1570 Cyprus emergency — ~100 galleys to sea in roughly two months (Frederic Lane's archival study is the scholarly anchor); 1574: a galley assembled, armed, and launched during Henry III's banquet — attested in contemporary visit accounts, though the "one hour" framing is the ceremonial best case, and "one ship a day" was a surge rate, not steady state. Dante namechecks the Arsenal (*Inferno* XXI, ~1320). [snippet-only: Lane via archive.org listing, worldhistory.org, historynet]
MECHANISM: The distinct bit isn't flow (lean covers that) — it's *capacity stored as kitted standard parts*: the reserve fleet existed as inventoried components + a rehearsed assembly sequence, so surge capacity was bought in advance and proven by demonstration.
PAIRING: Weak-to-moderate — TPS/standard-work overlap is heavy. Salvageable angle: incident-surge kits (pre-built templates, queries, comms) so a crisis response is assembly, not fabrication. Honestly: lowest distinctness on this list.
LLM AMPLIFICATION: Building and maintaining the kit (current runbooks, prefilled templates) is exactly the upkeep that rots; LLM keeps kits regenerated and demo-tested.
TRIGGER: "surge," "pre-staged," "how fast could we stand one up" — but flag collision risk with lean/standard-work skills.

**7. Cathedral multi-generation continuity** — Gothic Europe, 12th–16th c.
DOCUMENTED: The surprise is what *didn't* carry the design: almost no architectural drawings survive pre-1200; parchment plans (Strasbourg, Reims palimpsest) appear only from mid-13th c. What actually transmitted intent: the built fabric itself, full-scale (1:1) plaster tracing floors at York and Wells where each generation's master drew moldings at execution scale, and wooden templates cut from those tracings and handed to stonecutters — direct replication, no abstraction. Villard de Honnecourt's "lodge book" is now read by scholars as a personal portfolio, *not* a design-transmission document. [snippet-only: drawingmatter.org, Cambridge tracing-house paper (Holton), Wikipedia/academia on Villard]
MECHANISM: Continuity ran on *executable artifacts at working scale* (templates you cut stone against) plus the standing work-in-progress as its own spec — not on intent prose. Where docs are thin, make the artifact and its jigs the documentation.
PAIRING (pre-directive): multi-year projects across contractor generations: prefer runnable templates — seeded configs, golden-path scripts, worked examples — over intent memos; treat the production system as a readable spec and annotate it in place.
LLM AMPLIFICATION: Extracting the implicit "template" from an existing system (why is this rule shaped this way?) is archaeology humans skip; LLMs do it well and can regenerate the 1:1 worked example each generation.
TRIGGER: "the person who set this up is gone," "handover artifacts," "spec vs working example," "project will outlive us."

**8. Khipu + quipucamayoc cross-checking** — Inka Empire, ~1400–1560s.
DOCUMENTED: Knotted-cord decimal records at every bureaucratic level; Garcilaso de la Vega reports a minimum of four khipu keepers per community keeping the *same* accounts; Gary Urton's work (Cambridge, *Latin American Antiquity*) identifies actual surviving matching/duplicate khipu pairs and triples — physical evidence of parallel redundant encoding. [snippet-only: cambridge.org (Urton), Harvard Library, Britannica]
MECHANISM: Verification by *independent parallel derivation* — multiple keepers encode the same events separately from source reality, and agreement is checked record-against-record; N-version bookkeeping rather than after-the-fact matching of two systems' outputs.
PAIRING: Honest call — WEAK. Reconciliation is deeply covered, and the residual angle (a second, independent derivation of a critical number) is real but thin; entry #2's tally sticks deliver the sharper version of "independent evidence."
LLM AMPLIFICATION: The LLM *is* the cheap fourth quipucamayoc — an independent re-derivation from raw source on demand.
TRIGGER: "re-derive from raw," "second keeper," "does an independent calc agree" — only if not folded into #2.

**Strongest pick:** Wayfinding/etak — highest distinctness from the covered list, direct value (position estimation when feeds fail), and fully documented, no legend-laundering needed.
**Most surprising documented fact:** The "Roman engineer under the arch" traces to a ~2004 USENET signature line, while the 3,800-year-old Hammurabi text is verbatim real — the library's perfect provenance parable; runner-up: Britain's Parliament burned down disposing of its own 650-year-old internal-control artifacts.
**Weakest cut:** Khipu (fold its re-derivation angle into split-tally/verification) — and if only one more must go, the Arsenal, whose mechanism mostly re-derives lean/standard-work already on the shelf.

## Lane 2 — Fiction-derived frameworks with documented professional adoption

# Fiction-Derived Frameworks — Research Findings (ranked best-first)

## 1. KOBAYASHI MARU — the no-win scenario as training method ★ STRONGEST
**CHARACTER/STORY:** Starfleet Academy's unwinnable rescue simulation; Kirk beats it by reprogramming the test (*Star Trek II*, 1982).
**DOCUMENTED PRACTICE:** Two independently documented blades. (a) No-win simulation in medical education: [Annals of Emergency Medicine study on "no-win patient scenario"](https://www.annemergmed.com/article/S0196-0644(14)00652-0/fulltext) [peer-reviewed] — patient-death scenarios yield greater educational benefit than survivable twins; [EMS "Kobayashi Maru" stress-training concept](https://www.academia.edu/10212226/EMS_Stress_Training_Concept_Kobayashi_Maru_Scenarios) [practitioner]; debriefing-centered use in [healthcare simulation](https://www.healthysimulation.com/the-kobayashi-maru-lessons-on-simulation-from-star-trek/) [industry]. (b) Sanctioned rule-breaking in cyber education: Conti (West Point) & Caroland (US Cyber Command), ["Embracing the Kobayashi Maru: Why You Should Teach Your Students to Cheat," IEEE Security & Privacy 2011](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2a0b3776f80e3a8cb791a581e3c91efaf81b9f33) [peer-reviewed, military-academy provenance] — students assigned an exam they could only pass by cheating, to teach adversarial reframing.
**MECHANISM:** Remove the winning move, then grade the *decision process*: loss-minimization, explicit triage of what to save, communication under futility, and post-hoc debrief of emotional response. Blade (b): before accepting a constraint set, ask "which constraints are physics and which are policy?" — reframing is legitimate when it changes policy constraints transparently and owns the consequences; it's cheating when it hides the change or fakes the outcome.
**DISTINCTNESS:** Wargaming plays an adversary toward winning; pre-mortem imagines failure causes. Neither trains *choosing among only-bad options* or *auditing the test's own frame*. Genuinely uncovered.
**PAIRING (pre-directive):** validation-design (blade b is its mirror: when is beating the test legitimate?); tabletop-wargaming (add a no-win variant round); degraded-mode operations under a standing deadline; decisions where every option costs.
**LLM AMPLIFICATION:** LLM generates the scenario *guaranteeing* no clean exit (humans unconsciously leave doors open), plays the escalating environment, and runs the structured debrief separating decision quality from outcome. Also flags when the user is quietly redefining success mid-scenario — the Kirk move — and forces them to declare it.
**TRIGGER SKETCH:** "every option is bad," "we can't win this one," "least-worst," "damage control," "walk me through the disaster drill," "degraded mode," "no-win." Avoid bare "triage" — in the dental-app context it collides with clinical patient triage; use "loss triage" / "least-worst decision."

## 2. COLUMBO — disarming elicitation & the exit question
**CHARACTER/STORY:** Lt. Columbo (NBC, 1971–2003): rumpled underestimation, naive questions, "just one more thing" at the door.
**DOCUMENTED PRACTICE:** Named technique in three professions. Motivational interviewing's "Columbo approach" — deploying discrepancy non-judgmentally — is a staple of clinical MI training ([social-work MI training](https://www.socialworkshorts.co.uk/2022/01/28/motivational-interviewing-be-like-columbo/), [MI strategies handbook](https://peqatac.org/download/MI_Strategies_and_Techniques.pdf)) [clinical-pedagogy]. FBI counterintelligence documents "feigned ignorance" as a formal elicitation technique ([fbi.gov elicitation brochure](https://www.fbi.gov/file-repository/elicitation-brochure.pdf)) [gov]. Internal-investigations training teaches Columbo-style questioning ([Case IQ](https://www.caseiq.com/resources/what-columbo-can-teach-us-about-internal-investigations)) [industry]; tech-writer knowledge elicitation ([Ferri Benedetti](https://passo.uno/the-columbo-technique-for-technical-writers/)) [practitioner]. The exit-question blade has a peer-reviewed clinical twin: the ["doorknob phenomenon"](https://www.aafp.org/pubs/afp/issues/2018/0701/p52.html) [peer-reviewed, AAFP] — the most important disclosure comes when the interview seems over; remedied by trained "anything else?" prompts.
**MECHANISM:** Lower the stakes so the expert educates rather than defends: signal harmlessness, ask "help me understand" questions, restate their words slightly wrong so they correct you, hold contradictions as *your* confusion ("what I can't figure out is...") not their fault. Then the doorknob move: after formal close, one casual question — post-closure answers are the least rehearsed.
**DISTINCTNESS:** Kaizen-codesign covers *what* to ask (VOC); nothing covers the *stance* that makes experts volunteer what they forgot they know. Principled-negotiation is interest-based bargaining, not elicitation.
**PAIRING (pre-directive):** kaizen-and-codesign (VOC interviews with dental staff — surfacing tacit workflow); ui-and-ux-inspection (user interviews); non-defensive walkthroughs; eliciting undocumented tribal knowledge; requirements sessions generally.
**LLM AMPLIFICATION:** LLM drafts the question sequence (naive → restatement-with-error → discrepancy-held-as-own-confusion → doorknob), role-plays the defensive expert for rehearsal, and audits interview transcripts for missed doorknob moments and questions that triggered defensiveness.
**TRIGGER SKETCH:** "stakeholder interview," "requirements gathering," "the users can't articulate what they do," "expert won't open up," "walkthrough with the process owner." "One more thing" as a cute alias — fine as a nickname, not the primary trigger.

## 3. SELDON CRISIS → break-glass & trigger-armed playbooks
**CHARACTER/STORY:** Asimov's *Foundation*: Hari Seldon pre-records guidance that unseals when predicted crises arrive — the answer was prepared when heads were cool.
**DOCUMENTED PRACTICE (the real anchor):** Break-glass emergency-access procedures — documented, tested, pre-staged accounts required in healthcare ([Yale HIPAA break-glass procedure](https://hipaa.yale.edu/security/break-glass-procedure-granting-emergency-access-critical-ephi-systems)) [gov/compliance] and mapped to [NIST SP 800-53 / CIS 17 testing requirements](https://hoop.dev/blog/break-glass-access-under-the-nist-cybersecurity-framework/) [framework]. Treasury twin: bank Contingency Funding Plans — regulator-mandated playbooks with early-warning indicators, graduated stress triggers, and pre-approved action menus ([Fed SR 10-6](https://www.federalreserve.gov/boarddocs/srletters/2010/sr1006.htm), [2023 interagency update](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/files/bcreg20230728a1.pdf)) [regulatory].
**MECHANISM:** For each foreseeable crisis: define the tripwire metric (not vibes — a number), pre-author the first 10 moves and who may execute them, pre-grant the emergency authority *with* automatic expiry and full logging, and test the unsealing quarterly. The design question is "what must already exist when the alarm sounds?"
**DISTINCTNESS:** Pre-mortem finds the failure modes; this is the *output stage* pre-mortem lacks — converting each imagined failure into an armed, testable playbook. BCP/wargaming rehearse; this pre-positions authority and instructions.
**PAIRING (pre-directive):** pre-mortem (natural downstream); deploy-and-operate (break-glass admin, kill switches, rollback runbooks for the dental app); breach/incident playbooks.
**LLM AMPLIFICATION:** LLM converts pre-mortem output into tripwire+playbook pairs, red-checks each trigger for measurability ("who watches this number, how often?"), drafts the sealed instructions at calm-headed quality, and simulates the unsealing drill.
**TRIGGER SKETCH:** "what do we do if/when X hits," "emergency access," "runbook," "if the feed goes down at quarter-end," "kill switch," "we'd be scrambling."

## 4. ULYSSES PACT — self-binding against predictable bad judgment
**CHARACTER/STORY:** Odysseus lashed to the mast — orders his crew to refuse his own future commands (*Odyssey*).
**DOCUMENTED PRACTICE:** "Ulysses contracts"/self-binding directives are an established instrument in psychiatry — patients pre-authorize treatment they know they'll refuse mid-crisis ([PubMed: psychiatric advance directives as Ulysses Contract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37807870/), [Ulysses clause review, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3512354/)) [peer-reviewed, legally instantiated in several jurisdictions]; commitment devices are core behavioral economics.
**MECHANISM:** In a calm state, identify the situations where your future judgment will predictably degrade (deadline pressure, sunk costs, market panic, 11pm production incident), then bind yourself *now*: hard rules, required second signatures, cooling-off periods — and pre-decide what evidence would legitimately unbind you (else the pact is a straitjacket).
**DISTINCTNESS:** Sunk-cost revision review (the-challenger) revisits commitments; Ulysses *creates* binding ones against a foreseen worse self. Complements Seldon (Seldon pre-positions action; Ulysses pre-positions restraint).
**PAIRING (pre-directive):** stop rules; dual approval as institutional mast-lashing; git-and-code-review ("no force-push after 6pm; no schema change on close day"); agentic-workflow-design (guardrails written in calm mode that the agent may not renegotiate under pressure).
**LLM AMPLIFICATION:** LLM interviews for degradation scenarios, drafts the pact with unbinding criteria, and — uniquely — *enforces* it later: "you adopted a rule against exactly this on June 3; here's what you said."
**TRIGGER SKETCH:** "I always cave when...," "stop us from overriding," "we broke our own rule again," "commit ourselves now," "future-proof this decision against panic."

## 5. CHESTERTON'S FENCE — verdict: rule absorbed; a narrow removal protocol survives
The principle itself is fully absorbed into existing skills — [software usage is pervasive but is a maxim, not a method](https://josephwoodward.co.uk/2020/04/software-the-chestertons-fence-principle) [practitioner]. However, one documented operational descendant exists: the **scream test** — disable the suspect system, wait a defined window, see who screams, then decommission — with named industry practice at [Microsoft (Inside Track: ~15% of "unused" servers screamed)](https://www.microsoft.com/insidetrack/blog/microsoft-uses-a-scream-test-to-silence-its-unused-servers/) and [Uptime Institute decommissioning programs](https://journal.uptimeinstitute.com/decommissioning-discipline-server-roundup-winners-share-success/) [industry]. Honest call: not a standalone skill; a "safe-removal protocol" section (archaeology → scream test → reversibility window → delete) folded into an existing review skill. MECHANISM: make removal an experiment with a rollback, not a decision.

## 6. MURPHY'S LAW — verdict: cut (legend salvage only)
Origin verified real, not fiction: Capt. Edward Murphy, [Edwards AFB MX981 rocket-sled program, 1949; Stapp promoted it as safety philosophy](https://www.military.com/history/real-life-murphy-and-how-murphys-law-came-be.html) [historical]. The method residue — "if it can be installed backwards, it will be; design so it can't" — is exactly poka-yoke/defensive design, covered by fmea + standard-work + checklist-design. No distinct blade. Salvage: the Murphy/Stapp story is a superb *legend anchor* if fmea or checklist-design ever wants a house-style origin story (the accelerometers that *could* be wired backwards were — the fix was connectors that only fit one way).

## 7. RING OF GYGES — verdict: cut (absorbed)
Documented only as ethics pedagogy ([business-ethics curricula, Markkula Center](https://www.scu.edu/ethics/the-power-of-our-voices/lessons-from-plato-and-the-ring-of-gyges/)) [pedagogy], not as an engineering method. Its design translation — "what does this system permit when nobody is watching?" — is one review question, already covered (audit trail, SOD, "the system is an actor"). Fold in as a named lens ("Gyges check") at most.

## 8. SCHEHERAZADE — verdict: cut (weakest)
Cliffhanger/serialized narrative is documented in *fiction publishing and media* ([Victorian serialization, Dickens](https://wordsliketrees.wordpress.com/2019/04/07/anatomy-of-a-cliffhanger/)) [literary-historical], but no credible professional practice of cliffhanger-structured status reporting surfaced — business appropriations are generic "storytelling" seminars [anecdotal]. Directly loses to adams-smart-brevity, which is evidence-backed and already in-house. Nothing to salvage.

**Strongest pick:** Kobayashi Maru — two peer-reviewed blades (EM patient-death simulation; IEEE S&P sanctioned-cheating pedagogy from West Point/US Cyber Command), zero library overlap, direct degraded-mode/deadline day-job value.
**Most surprising documented adoption:** Columbo's exit move has an independent peer-reviewed clinical twin — the AAFP "doorknob phenomenon" — meaning "just one more thing" is literally trained-for in family medicine.
**Weakest cuts:** Scheherazade (no professional practice, loses to Smart Brevity), Murphy (fully absorbed by FMEA/poka-yoke; keep only as legend material), Gyges (one review question, already covered).

# Biological/physical systems — Research Findings (ranked best-first)

## 1. IMMUNE SYSTEM → detection-system tuning between autoimmunity and immunodeficiency ★ TOP PICK
**DOCUMENTED SCIENCE/PRACTICE [snippet-only; heavily convergent]:**
- **Artificial immune systems (AIS) is a real 30-year engineering literature, not metaphor:** four algorithm families — negative selection (Forrest, self/non-self), clonal selection, immune networks, danger theory/dendritic-cell algorithm (Aickelin, from Matzinger's danger model) — with documented intrusion/anomaly-detection deployments and surveys ([AIS survey](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1006.4949), [negative-selection survey](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1574013723000242)).
- **The literature documents the exact failure trade the skill needs:** Stibor et al. (GECCO 2005) showed negative selection suffers false-positive and detector-scaling problems — the "autoimmunity" mode formalized ([Stibor](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1068009.1068061)); danger theory exists *specifically* to cut false positives by requiring context ("damage signals"), not just non-self — i.e., alert only when anomaly co-occurs with harm evidence.
- **Alarm fatigue evidence — the numbers:** Joint Commission Sentinel Event Alert 50 (Apr 2013): **98 alarm-related adverse events Jan 2009–Jun 2012, 80 deaths, 13 permanent loss of function; 85–99% of alarm signals need no clinical intervention** ([KFF](https://kffhealthnews.org/news/sounding-an-alarm-on-alarms/), [HCPLive](https://www.hcplive.com/view/recommendations-to-reduce-alarm-fatigue-issued-by-joint-commission)). Documented fix with numbers: Boston Medical Center recalibrated *default* alarm limits → **89% reduction in audible alarms (12,546→1,424/day) in pilot, 60% hospital-wide across 310 telemetry beds**, no missed events reported ([ECRI](https://www.ecri.org/components/HDJournal/Pages/HD-Achievement-Award-Boston-Medical.aspx?PF=1)).
- **SOC mirror:** false-positive rates >50–80% in enterprise SOCs; 67% of analysts can't clear daily alerts; 63–76% report burnout; ~3 hrs/day manual triage ([ACM Computing Surveys 2025 on SOC alert fatigue](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3723158), [CyberDefenders](https://cyberdefenders.org/blog/soc-alert-fatigue/)). **Banking twin: PwC-attributed benchmark that 90–95% of AML transaction-monitoring alerts are false positives**, $25–50 manual cost per alert ([Facctum](https://www.facctum.com/blog/aml-false-positive-report), [Unit21](https://www.unit21.ai/blog/reduce-false-positives-in-aml-transaction-monitoring)). Incident-derived memory is documented SOC practice: detection-as-code, postmortem→Sigma rule lifecycle ([Splunk](https://www.splunk.com/en_us/blog/learn/detection-as-code.html)).
**MECHANISM:** Any detection system sits on an axis between autoimmunity (attacks self = false matches, junk exceptions, alarm floods → operator desensitization, the *real* documented killer) and immunodeficiency (misses non-self). Operate it layered — cheap innate screens first, costly adaptive investigation second; require a *danger signal* (context/damage evidence), not mere anomaly, before paging a human; induce tolerance for verified-benign recurring patterns (suppress at the source, like BMC's defaults); and convert every true incident into a memory cell (a permanent cheap detector) so second exposure is fast and specific.
**PAIRING (pre-directive):** exception queues and match tuning; `full-stack-dev-skills:deploy-and-operate` — dental-app alerting gets the danger-signal page gate + BMC default-recalibration move. Handoff seam: `machine-learning-skills:anomaly-detection` owns *building* detectors; this skill owns *operating and tuning the whole layered system* — queue economics, disposition feedback, memory formation — which no existing skill covers (SPC covers process stability, not alert-queue tuning).
**LLM AMPLIFICATION:** The LLM reads a quarter of exception-queue dispositions and computes the empirical autoimmunity rate per rule; drafts the tolerance-induction list (recurring benign patterns with evidence counts); converts incident postmortems into memory-cell detection rules; simulates a proposed threshold change against historical alerts before it goes live.
**TRIGGER SKETCH:** Never bare "alert" or bare "false positive" (anomaly-detection/model-evaluation territory) and never bare "tolerance". Safe: "alert fatigue", "alarm fatigue", "immune", "autoimmunity", "detection tuning", "exception queue drowning", "too many false matches", "tune the alerts", "memory cell". "Immune system" appears nowhere in the library — safe to own.

## 2. STRATIGRAPHY / ARCHAEOLOGY → legacy-config excavation
**DOCUMENTED SCIENCE/PRACTICE [snippet-only; convergent]:**
- **Harris matrix is a real, standardized recording method** (Edward Harris, 1973/1979, *Principles of Archaeological Stratigraphy*): a directed graph of before/after/contemporary relationships between contexts, with laws (superposition, original horizontality…) and dedicated software (Harris Matrix Composer) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harris_matrix), [TU Wien HMC paper](https://www.cg.tuwien.ac.at/research/publications/2008/TRA08/TRA08-paper.pdf)).
- **Direct documented transfer to software exists:** Reinhard, "Adapting the Harris Matrix for Software Stratigraphy," *Advances in Archaeological Practice* (Cambridge, 2018) — treats a program (case study: No Man's Sky's patch history) as a digital site obeying Harris's laws, building a stratigraphic matrix from version layers ([Cambridge Core](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-archaeological-practice/article/abs/adapting-the-harris-matrix-for-software-stratigraphy/B5B4DC59B20ABCE3B86A6A4FEA640AF6)).
- **"Software archaeology" is a named practice:** Hunt & Thomas, *IEEE Software* 19(2), Mar/Apr 2002 — preserve artifacts, record as you dig, respect the cultural forces that produced the code ([IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/991327/)); OOPSLA 2001 workshop lineage ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_archaeology)).
**MECHANISM:** Before demolishing, excavate: record every artifact with its stratigraphic relationships — who references whom (cuts/abuts), created/modified dates and naming-convention eras (dating), which rules fire above others (superposition) — into a Harris-matrix DAG of configuration "eras." Only then classify layers as living floor, fill, or rubble. The discipline is *record-before-remove* with an explicit relationship graph, which RCA/VSM don't provide.
**PAIRING (pre-directive):** any accreted legacy system — configs, codebases, a mature product's vocabulary strata. Distinct from root-cause-analysis: RCA explains one failure; excavation reconstructs the whole deposit's history so *safe demolition* is possible (Chesterton's fence, systematized).
**LLM AMPLIFICATION:** The killer app — the LLM reads a thousand config rows + naming patterns + the reference graph and emits the Harris matrix in minutes (archaeologists spend seasons on this); clusters rows into named eras; flags "still-inhabited" layers (referenced by live rules) vs. safe rubble.
**TRIGGER SKETCH:** Safe and fun: "software archaeology", "excavate", "dig into this legacy", "Harris matrix", "stratigraphy", "which of these are dead". Avoid bare "legacy" (too broad) and bare "layer"/"setup".

## 3. EPIDEMIOLOGY OF DEFECTS → contact-tracing for copy-paste propagation (narrow)
**DOCUMENTED SCIENCE/PRACTICE [snippet-only; convergent]:** Clone-vulnerability propagation is an established research line with tooling: **ReDeBug** found unpatched code clones across entire OS distributions (IEEE S&P 2012 — patches routinely fail to propagate to clones, leaving latent vulnerabilities) ([paper PDF](https://www.ieee-security.org/TC/SP2012/papers/4681a048.pdf)); **VUDDY** scaled vulnerable-clone discovery with function fingerprints (IEEE S&P 2017) ([PDF](https://seulbae-security.github.io/pubs/vuddy-sp17.pdf)); **Juergens et al., "Do Code Clones Matter?" (ICSE 2009)** — inconsistent changes to clones are frequent and yielded ~107 confirmed faults across commercial/OSS systems ([ACM](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1109/ICSE.2009.5070547)). Clone genealogy research (Kim et al.) documents lineage tracking.
**MECHANISM:** When one bug is confirmed, treat it as an index case, not a singleton: fingerprint the defective pattern, contact-trace every clone (including mutated variants), check each contact's patch status, and find patient zero (the origin commit/template) to stop reinfection at the source. R0 = average clones spawned per instance of a pattern — high-R0 templates deserve quarantine (fix the template, lint-block the pattern).
**PAIRING (pre-directive):** systematizes `coding-agent-skills:chicken-little-college-kid`'s template-clone warning (dental app); pairs with `coding-agent-skills:git-and-code-review` (a found-bug review step: "trace the contacts before closing"). Distinct from RCA (one causal chain, one incident) and FMEA (prospective, mode-by-mode): this is *retrospective population-level spread control* — the unit is the pattern, not the failure.
**LLM AMPLIFICATION:** LLM does semantic clone detection cheaply (catches Type-3/4 mutated clones grep misses), builds the transmission tree from git history, computes per-template R0, drafts the quarantine lint rule.
**TRIGGER SKETCH:** Safe: "contact tracing", "patient zero", "code clone", "copy-paste bug", "everywhere else this appears", "outbreak". Avoid bare "clone" (git clone collision) and bare "spread".

## 4. ECOLOGICAL SUCCESSION / DISTURBANCE REGIMES — verdict: fold-in, don't build
**Documented [snippet-only; strong]:** Fire-suppression paradox is rigorously documented — suppression removes mild fires, accumulates fuel, and concentrates burning into extreme conditions ("suppression bias"), Nature Communications 2024 ([paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-46702-0)); prescribed burning is established USFS practice. Ops counterpart exists and is documented: Google SRE **error budgets** — failures within budget are *deliberately accepted* so risk surfaces continuously instead of accumulating ([SRE book](https://sre.google/sre-book/embracing-risk/)).
**But:** deliberate small disturbances = chaos engineering (covered); tolerated-failure economics = error budgets (belong in deploy-and-operate/stability patterns). The only distinct residue — fuel-load accumulation as a *model of suppressed-backlog risk* (every auto-suppressed exception, deferred cleanup, and force-matched line is fuel) — is one section, not a skill: a "fuel load" reference inside the immune-system skill's tolerance chapter (tolerance induction without periodic re-validation *is* fuel accumulation).

## 5. MYCORRHIZAL NETWORKS — verdict: cut
Metaphor-only — and the source science itself is now contested: Karst, Jones & Hoeksema, *Nature Ecology & Evolution* 2023, reviewed ~1,500 papers and found the field's headline claims (widespread forest common-mycorrhizal networks, preferential resource transfer to kin) unsupported or overinterpreted, with positive-citation bias doubling unsupported claims over 25 years ([paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-023-01986-1), [SciAm](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/do-trees-support-each-other-through-a-network-of-fungi/)). No documented engineering transfer found. Its one usable artifact is methodological: the citation-bias finding is a ready-made cautionary exhibit for `deep-research-skills:medical-research-detective`'s claim-verification stage.

**Strongest:** Immune system — a real algorithm literature (AIS) plus quantified alarm-fatigue evidence (80 deaths; 85–99% non-actionable; BMC's 89% cut via defaults; AML's 90–95% FP benchmark) lands on detection-tuning problems in a gap no existing skill occupies.
**Most surprising evidence:** Reinhard 2018 in a Cambridge archaeology journal literally built a Harris matrix for software — the stratigraphy transfer is peer-reviewed practice, not our invention; and BMC fixed alarm fatigue not with smarter alerts but by recalibrating *defaults* (a pure tuning move).
**Weakest cut:** Mycorrhizal networks — metaphor-only atop biology now shown overinterpreted (Karst 2023); fire ecology survives only as a "fuel load" section inside the immune skill, its mechanism otherwise absorbed by chaos engineering and error budgets.
