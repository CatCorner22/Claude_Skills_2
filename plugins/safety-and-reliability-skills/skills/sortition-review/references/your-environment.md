# Your environment (sanitized template)

Wire in your current role here. This skill is domain-neutral by design — the population
can be approvals, changes, expense lines, filings, closed tickets, or anything else
that flows through your process, whoever you are: analyst, attorney, operations
manager, or developer. Keep the committed file **structural**: mechanisms and roles,
not names or live identifiers. Real names, system paths, seed values in use, and
anything sensitive go in `your-environment.private.md` — that suffix is git-ignored
and never committed.
