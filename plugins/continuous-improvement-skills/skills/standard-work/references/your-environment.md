Wire in your current role here. Standard work is domain-neutral by design — it attaches to
whatever repeatable tasks an analyst, attorney, ops manager, or developer owns, and it moves
with you to the next job. Keep this committed file **structural**: task kinds, conventions,
and cadences. Real systems, accounts, credentials, or people belong in
`your-environment.private.md` — that suffix is git-ignored and never committed.
