# Your tabletop environment (sanitized template)

Fill this in with your real setup. Anything naming real accounts, counterparties, people,
or live controls belongs in `your-environment.private.md` — that suffix is git-ignored.
An exercise file that leaks real controls is itself an incident; commit structure only.

- **Scenario priorities:** <which of the three starters (impersonated urgent instruction,
  critical-vendor outage on a deadline day, ransomware in a close/freeze) or others come
  first, and why>
- **The irreversible actions to drill:** <what your organization cannot take back — a
  payment release, an access grant, a bulk export, a filing, a public statement>
- **Grounding documents for injects:** <the runbooks, deadline calendars, contact trees,
  and approval procedures injects must be modeled on — sanitized names/locations>
- **Role roster:** <who plays blue by role; who can sit white cell; who owns each
  process step for adjudication questions>
- **Authority limits to test:** <who may halt the process, switch providers or channels,
  invoke the incident plan, speak externally>
- **Red-cell bounds:** <what the adversary may and may not do in your exercises>
- **Exercise cadence and filing:** <how often, where turn logs and AAR records go>
- **Standing intent drafts:** <purpose/key-tasks/end-state for your recurring scenarios,
  kept current with the process owner>
