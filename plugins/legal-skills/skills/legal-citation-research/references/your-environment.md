# Your environment — legal-citation-research (template)

Fill this in for your current role, and keep the filled copy outside the plugin at
`.claude/skills-env/legal-citation-research.private.md` (git-ignored by the `*.private.*`
pattern — confirm your own repository ignores it too). Nothing identifying a client or a matter
belongs in the shipped file, and the CourtListener token belongs in neither: keep it in the
`COURTLISTENER_API_TOKEN` environment variable or a token file outside any repository.

## Jurisdictions and style
- Jurisdictions you practise in, and for each: the citation style that governs (Bluebook, ALWD,
  state manual), the local rules on citing unpublished opinions, and any standing orders on
  generative-tool disclosure or citation certification (court, judge, date checked).

## Databases and citator
- Licensed: Westlaw / Lexis / Fastcase / other — and which citator (KeyCite / Shepard's).
- Free: CourtListener (token set: yes/no), Google Scholar, court sites you use for slip opinions.
- Official statutory and regulatory sources for your states.

## Memo conventions
- Heading format, length limits, who receives the ledger, whether confidence is stated in words
  or a scale, file-naming.

## Recurring questions
- The questions that come back every quarter, with the authority last used and the date it was
  last checked for currency — so the next pass starts from the ledger rather than from zero.
