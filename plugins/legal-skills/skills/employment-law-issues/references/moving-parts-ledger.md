## §5 How to re-check

1. Open the primary source named in the route column, not an article about it.
2. Read the effective date and any note on amendments, stays or vacatur.
3. Write the figure, the source, and today's date in your `your-environment.md`.
4. If the item needs an authority in a memo, run it through
   `legal-skills:legal-citation-research` so the citation is verified and the ledger carries it.
