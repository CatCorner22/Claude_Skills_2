# Lifecycle issue checklist — issues, sources, what to verify, what to pull, counsel triggers

Every number is marked. **[rule text]** means the figure is in the named statute or regulation
and is stable enough to state, but open the source before relying on it. **[snippet-only]** means
it was confirmed from published summaries on the date shown, not from the instrument.
**[background — verify]** means general knowledge stated for orientation. State law is listed by
topic, not by figure, because the figures differ by state and change yearly; the verification
route is the state's labor department and statute.
