## §1 The issue-list template

```
**HR issue list -- [situation in six words] -- [date], [states involved]**

**Exposure:** [The single biggest issue in one sentence, with its rough size: a claim, a penalty,
a pattern.]

**Why it matters:** [What happens if nothing is done, in one sentence -- a deadline, a claim that
accrues, a precedent for the rest of the workforce.]

**Do now:**
- [Preserve / hold / interim measure]
- [Stop a clock: notice, final pay, revocation period]

**Decisions needed:**
- [Decision] -- owner, by [date]

**Issues found:**
- [Issue] -- [governing source, named] -- [exposure: low / medium / high] -- [status]
- ...

**Verify before relying:**
- [Number or position] -- [source to open] -- [checked DATE / not yet]

**Counsel triggers present:** [list, or "none"]

**Not an issue, and why:** [what was considered and cleared]

---
Ledger
| Issue | Source (layer) | Verified (how, date) | Status |
```

## §2 The document-review table

For an agreement, a handbook or a policy:

| § | Provision | Issue | Fix | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | confidentiality and non-disparagement | broad, no carve-outs; non-supervisory signer | add agency-charge, whistleblower and § 7 carve-outs; narrow to trade secrets and defamation | NLRA § 7; SEC Rule 21F-17; *McLaren Macomb* (under reconsideration — ledger §2) | **flag — counsel** |
| 9 | release | no § 1542 language; employee works in California | add the statutory waiver | Cal. Civ. Code § 1542 [rule text] | edit |
| 12 | consideration period | 14 days; employee is 52 | 21 days, 7-day revocation, advice-of-counsel sentence, ADEA named | 29 U.S.C. § 626(f) [rule text] | edit |

Wording goes to `legal-skills:mscd-contract-drafting`; the *Source* column goes through
`legal-skills:legal-citation-research` before it is cited.

## §3 Worked example

Situation: a 30-person company in New York terminated a sales employee for missed targets on
October 10; the employee had emailed HR on September 2 asking whether her unpaid overtime was
lawful; she is 44; a separation agreement with a 14-day consideration period and a broad
non-disparagement clause was offered.

> **HR issue list — termination five weeks after an overtime inquiry — 2026-10-14, NY**
>
> **Exposure:** a retaliation claim under the FLSA and New York law, independent of whether the
> targets were missed, because the termination followed a wage inquiry by five weeks.
>
> **Why it matters:** the inquiry itself was protected activity; the decision needs documentation
> that predates September 2, and the agreement as drafted would not protect the company and may
> itself be a problem.
>
> **Do now:**
> - Preserve the sales records, the target communications and the September 2 email thread.
> - Withdraw the 14-day agreement before it is signed; reissue under §3 of the playbook.
> - Confirm final pay was made within the state's timing rule — verify the rule, not assumed.
>
> **Decisions needed:**
> - Whether to offer severance at all, and how much — owner, by Friday.
> - Whether the overtime question is answered correctly for the whole sales team — HR, this month.
>
> **Issues found:**
> - Retaliation timing — FLSA § 215(a)(3); NY Labor Law — high — open.
> - Misclassification of inside sales as exempt — FLSA part 541 (salary level $684/week as
>   restored; verify) and NY's higher salary level (verify) — medium — open.
> - OWBPA defects: 14 days, no advice-of-counsel sentence, ADEA not named — 29 U.S.C. § 626(f)
>   [rule text] — high (release unenforceable as to age claims) — fix.
> - Non-disparagement without carve-outs — NLRA § 7; SEC 21F-17; Board precedent under
>   reconsideration — medium — flag for counsel.
>
> **Verify before relying:** NY final-pay timing; NY exempt salary level for 2026; the current
> FLSA level at 29 C.F.R. § 541.600; the Board's current severance standard.
>
> **Counsel triggers present:** protected-activity timing; a release with age-claim defects.
>
> **Not an issue, and why:** WARN — a single termination; COBRA — the company is above 20, notice
> timing to be confirmed by the administrator.

## §4 Rules of the form

Exposure before anything; one sentence each for exposure and why it matters; every issue carries
its source and a status; every number carries its source and a check date or "not yet"; the
counsel triggers are listed even when the answer is none; what was cleared is stated so the reader
knows it was considered. The ledger reconciles to the body.
