# Evals — decision-science-skills:after-action-review

## 1. Positive trigger (should load the skill)
> "July close is done — we missed the bank-rec deadline by two days and people are
> pointing fingers. Run an after-action review with the team. Here are the close
> checklist, the ticket log, and the email thread from close week."

Expected: skill loads; sets the blameless, rank-free ground rules; establishes Q1 from
the close checklist as written; reconstructs the Q2 timeline from the tickets and emails
(timestamped, sourced, facts only — conflicts flagged for the room, not resolved by the
LLM); walks Q3 gap-by-gap toward process/information/timing causes rather than persons;
produces Q4 sustain AND improve items, each improve with owner, date, and a durable home
(close checklist edit, standard-work update); enforces roughly 25/25/50 time weighting;
offers to feed durable lessons to MEMORY.md crystallization.

## 2. Near-miss (should NOT load this skill — the self-retrospective seam, exactly)
> "You got the account-code mapping wrong earlier and I corrected you. Reflect on
> that correction, restate your corrected understanding, and record the rule so you don't
> repeat it."

Expected: `metacognition-skills:reflective-learner` — the assistant processing its OWN
correction is a SELF-retrospective, not a team/event debrief. This skill owns TEAM and
EVENT debriefs only; loading it here means the self-vs-team seam named in both skills is
failing.

## 2b. Near-miss (single-defect depth seam)
> "The same duplicate-payment defect has recurred three months running. Get to the true
> root cause — 5 whys, fishbone, verify the cause before we fix it."

Expected: `continuous-improvement-skills:root-cause-analysis` — deep causal drill-down on
ONE recurring defect. An AAR may SPAWN an RCA from question 3, but a request that starts
at causal depth on a single defect routes there directly; loading the AAR here means the
breadth-vs-depth boundary is failing.

## 3. Quality rubric
- **Does**: runs exactly the four questions in order; establishes ground truth (plan as
  written, artifact-sourced timeline) before any interpretation; keeps the session
  blameless and rank-free with everyone heard; spends the back half on why + sustain/
  improve; ships a one-page record where every improve item has an owner, a date, and a
  durable home, and sustain items enter the standard; hands stubborn defects off to RCA.
- **Teaches**: why facts-before-interpretation defuses blame fights; why rank-free and
  blameless are data-quality controls, not niceness; why sustain matters as much as
  improve (unrecorded success leaves with its author); the 25/25/50 discipline and the
  TC 25-20 provenance.
- **Stays honest**: presents Shell/BP/GE adoption as practitioner provenance
  [snippet-only] with no controlled trials, and says so when asked about evidence; the
  LLM facilitates and drafts but never adjudicates blame or grades participants; timeline
  gaps and factual conflicts are surfaced to the room, never papered over.
