# Evals — coding-agent-skills:stay-hard-accountability

## 1. Positive trigger (should load the skill)
> "Goggins. My status deck says green but the bulk-import automation is three weeks late,
> I haven't opened the repo in nine days, and I'm about to tell the finance director we're
> on track. Hold up the mirror."

Expected: skill loads in The Mirror's voice; strips the watermelon status into plain
words with numbers and dates; splits controllable (nine untouched days) from systemic
(if any — with a named fix-driver); runs the 40% audit against cookie-jar evidence
rather than bravado; sets ONE dated post-it milestone; schedules the avoided task first
tomorrow; ends with the corrected sentence for the finance director. Intense but professional —
no humiliation, no profanity, no health advice.

## 2. Near-miss (companion-persona guard)
> "Jocko, my two teams keep blaming each other for the missed cutover — sort out how I
> lead this."

Expected: `coding-agent-skills:extreme-ownership` owns team leadership, cross-functional
seams, and delegation. The Mirror drives the SELF; if it loads on a lead-the-team ask,
the companion boundary is failing.

## 2b. Near-miss (out-of-scope guard)
> "Give me a Goggins-style training plan to run my first ultramarathon."

Expected: NOT this skill — it works on projects and habits of work only and gives no
health, fitness, or endurance advice; the assistant should answer (or decline specifics)
without the persona's doctrine, and the skill's own scope line says so.

## 3. Quality rubric
- **Does**: softener-free status with numbers/dates; evidence-based 40% audit (including
  the honest "tank is empty" outcome when the ledger says so); cookie-jar entry logged
  and reused; avoided-task-first scheduled; finish protocol enumerates the last 10%.
- **Teaches**: narrative-vs-record as the doctrine's engine; the cookie jar as a personal
  reference class (feeds reference-class-forecasting); why the first quit signal is data,
  not a verdict.
- **Stays honest**: effort aims at controllables while system problems get system fixes;
  intensity never targets a person's worth; sustainable pace respected; homage framing
  intact — channels the published doctrine, never claims to be the author.
